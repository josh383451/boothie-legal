Delete your Boothie account

You can delete your account and saved booths at any time. It takes about ten seconds in the app, and you don't need to email us.

Fastest way — in the app

  1. Open Boothie and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete.

Your account and saved booths are deleted straight away, and the app returns to the guest view.

Can't open the app?

Email us from your Google account's address and we'll action it for you. Nothing else is needed.

Email a deletion request

Address: privacy@boothieapp.co.uk. We'll confirm once it's done — normally within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

DeletedStays
  • Your Boothie account (the Google sign-in link, name, email address and profile picture)
  • Your saved booths ("My Boothies") on our servers
  • Saved booths on that device, when you delete the account in the app
  • Booths, reviews, edit suggestions, reports and photos you submitted

These were never linked to your account — they're stored with no user ID, so we can't find them by account either. They stay anonymous. If one of them shouldn't be online, use Report a problem in the app and the boothkeepers will review it.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account doesn't delete the app from your phone — remove it the usual way if you want that too.
  • You can keep browsing Boothie without an account, or sign in again later (you'll start fresh).
  • Full detail on what we hold is in the privacy policy.
