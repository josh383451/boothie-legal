Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Boothie is a free map for finding and sharing photo booths across the UK. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We've kept it as plain and short as we honestly can.

The short version: we collect as little as possible. If you just browse and search, we hold nothing about you at all. Signing in with Google adds your name, email and picture — that's the most we ever hold, and signing in is only needed to post (booths, reviews, edits, reports). We never show ads, sell data, or make anything public until a human checks it, and nothing you post is linked to your account.

1. Who we are

Boothie ("we") is the data controller for the information described here.

Boothie is the trading name of Josh Richards, a sole trader established in England and Wales.

Boothie has not launched yet, so the business postal address is not published here. It will be added before the app is released. In the meantime you can reach us at privacy@boothieapp.co.uk about anything in this policy, including your data rights, and we'll reply within a month.

3. Location, and staying in control

Location is the most personal thing we touch, so it's opt-in on purpose:

  • The app works fully without location — you can always search a city, postcode or street instead.
  • We only ask for permission when you tap "near me", after explaining why.
  • While the map is open we show a dot for where you are, so you can see the booths around you. It updates only while Boothie is on screen.
  • We never use your location in the background. Once you leave the app, nothing keeps checking where you are.
  • Your location is used to search for nearby booths and is not saved on our servers.

Google Play Services performs the location lookup and the geocoding on our behalf; their handling of that data is governed by the Google Terms of Service.

4. Your content and moderation

Anything you post (booth submissions, reviews, edit suggestions, reports) lands in a pending queue that the public cannot see. A human moderator approves it before it appears on the map, and can reject it. We may remove content that breaks our rules. Please don't post personal details about yourself or anyone else in submissions or reviews — for example photos of people, or home addresses.

Reporting. Every booth has a "Report a problem" option (closed, wrong details, a photo that shouldn't be there, or anything else). Reports go to the boothkeepers, not to the public, and never show who sent them.

5. Who processes our data

We use Google's Firebase platform to run Boothie:

  • Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage hold the booth listings, reviews, edit queues, reports, submission photos and saved-favourite lists. These are hosted in Google's London (europe-west2) region.
  • Firebase Authentication runs the Google sign-in that lets you post. It runs on Google's global infrastructure (primarily the USA).
  • Cloud Functions (also europe-west2) runs the one job that deletes an account and its saved booths when you ask it to.
  • Google Maps Platform and Google Play Services provide the map, location fixes and geocoding described above.

Where data leaves the UK, Google transfers it under the safeguard of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Google's SCC page). We never sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers — there are no ad networks in the app at all.

This website

These pages are plain, static files. We set no cookies, run no analytics and load nothing from third parties — even the fonts are served from this domain rather than a font service. The one thing that leaves your browser is the research survey, and only if you fill it in and press send: the answers go to our own Cloud Firestore database (Google, listed above) and are stored with no identifier attached. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (including IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention; that processing is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

6. How long we keep things

  • Saved favourites stay on your device until you unsave them or clear the app's data. If you're signed in, the server copy is deleted with your account, or when you unsave the last booth.
  • Submissions, reviews, edits and reports are kept while the listing or review is live. They contain no user ID, so they stay anonymous even after an account is deleted. Rejected items are kept for moderation-record purposes and can be deleted on request.
  • Your account (Google name, email, picture and the saved-booths copy) is deleted straight away when you use Profile → Delete account, or when we action a deletion request sent to the address below.
  • Survey answers are kept while they're useful for planning, then deleted in bulk. They carry no identifier, so we can't select one person's answers on request — the only way to remove a specific set is to delete the whole lot.

7. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure);
  • stop or restrict how we use it (objection or restriction);
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere (portability).

Email privacy@boothieapp.co.uk. Because the content you post carries no user ID, tell us roughly what and when you submitted (or screenshot it) and we'll find it.

You can also clear most data yourself in seconds:

  • Profile → Delete account removes your account and saved booths straight away (anything you submitted stays anonymous).
  • Unsave your hearts one by one.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Deletion requests can also be made from outside the app at boothieapp.co.uk/delete-account.

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk — though we'd love the chance to fix things first, so please email us too.

8. Children and teens

Boothie is designed with teens in mind and is intended for people aged 13 and over (13 is the age at which you can consent to data processing yourself in the UK). We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13 — if you believe an under-13 has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Privacy-friendly choices are built in for everyone, not just teens: no public profiles, no private messaging, no social graph to follow people, location strictly opt-in, and moderation before anything you post is public.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and sits in access-controlled Google Cloud services. Our own access is limited to moderating the content you submit. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow good practice and keep the data we hold to a minimum precisely so there's less to protect.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we'll update the "last updated" date on this page and show the new version in the app. If a change is significant — for example adding a new kind of data, or a new feature that uses what we hold — we'll say so clearly in the app before it happens.
